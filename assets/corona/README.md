# Corona's hardware face

The images Corona's card draws its amp page and its cabinet page with
(design-system.md "Rendered parts"). All were rendered for Northlight in Blender 4.2
from models made for it, by the scripts in `scripts/render/corona`, which list how
each was made. The reflections are of a Poly Haven HDRI (Lythwood Lounge, CC0); the
grille cloth's weave is generated by `weave_texture.py`. Nothing in them comes from a
photograph or another product, and none of them carries any lettering: every legend
is drawn live by the app.

They are kept at twice the face's drawn size (1160 x 600), so they are sharp at 200%.
The plates with a shadow carry a 12 px margin (24 px in the file) on every side for
it, so each is placed 12 px up and left of its rectangle on the face.
