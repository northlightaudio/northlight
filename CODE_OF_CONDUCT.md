# Code of conduct

Northlight is made by people with different backgrounds, levels of experience and
ideas about how music software should work. This page says how we treat each other
in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere else you
speak for the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project, to C++ or
  to audio.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Explain why, and accept that a decision
  can go the other way.
- Give feedback that someone can act on, and take it the same way.
- Own your mistakes, and apologise to the people they affected.
- Respect people's time: search before you ask, and say when you will not finish
  something you took on.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Insults, personal attacks, and comments about someone's identity, body or
  background.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or an email, without
  their permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing a conversation, or repeating something after you
  have been asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, write to the maintainers at
**steve@leafshade.io**. Reports are read only by the maintainers, and are kept
confidential. If a maintainer is involved, say so and they will take no part in
handling it.

## What happens

The maintainers decide what to do, in proportion to what happened. That can be a
private word, a public warning, a temporary ban from the project's spaces, or a
permanent one. We will tell the person reported what they did and why we acted.
