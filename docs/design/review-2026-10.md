# Design review, October 2026

A pass over the whole window for harmony: whether controls belong to one family,
whether spacing follows the 4 px grid and the tokens, and whether controls sit *in*
something or float on a flat plane. Screens reviewed: arrange with audio, MIDI and FX
tracks; mixer; piano roll; editor with the channel strip; browser; status bar.

Status: **Fixed** landed with this review; **Open** is proposed and not yet done.

## The underlying problem

Most of the "floatiness" comes from one habit: controls are placed straight onto a
panel's flat `surface0` with gaps between them and nothing that groups them. The
channel strip fixed this for itself with wells, trays and the material, but the
shell never got the same treatment. Three rules would fix most of what follows:

1. **Every group of related controls sits in a container.** A tray (`surface1`, radius
   `md`, a 1 px `lineSubtle` rim, the material's groove inset at the top edge) for
   button groups; a well (`meterWell`, inner shadow) for readouts, meters and graphs.
   A control alone on `surface0` is the exception, and it should look like a ghost.
2. **One height per row.** Everything sharing a horizontal band has the same height
   and the same centre line. Mixed 28/32/36 px heights side by side read as accidental.
3. **Parallel things line up.** Strips, rows and cards that repeat must put the same
   part at the same coordinate, even when one of them lacks a part (reserve the space).

## Header bar (transport): the weakest area

| # | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | 22 controls float on `surface0` with only gaps to group them. The transport keys, mode toggles, readouts, tempo/signature, edit tools and panel toggles have no visible grouping, so the eye can't find the clusters. | Fixed: each cluster sits in a tray |
| H2 | Heights are mixed: transport keys 32 px, toggles 28, readouts 28, tempo 28 with its own border, and the signature combo is a different style again. Their top edges don't line up. | Fixed: one 32 px control height inside 40 px trays |
| H3 | Toggles have two unrelated looks: an "on" toggle is a filled accent box with a border, an "off" toggle is a bare icon. Next to each other (loop on, punch off) they look like different kinds of control. | Fixed: the tray gives every toggle the same footprint. The on state stays the accent fill. |
| H4 | The two readouts are separate wells of different widths (150 and 110 px) with different glows, and tempo and signature use the field style instead of the readout style. In most DAWs this is one display. | Fixed: bars, time, tempo and signature share one readout well with dividers |
| H5 | Gaps follow no rule: 12 px between transport keys, 8 between toggles, 24 between groups, and the divider before the panel toggles is the only divider. | Fixed: 4 px tray padding, 2 px between controls in a tray, 12 px between trays |
| H6 | The bar has no lower edge. It runs into the arrange toolbar and the browser header with the same colour. | Fixed: a `lineSubtle` rule along the bottom |
| H7 | The project name is a ghost button with an unsaved dot drawn by hand, between the app mark and undo. It reads as a stray label. | Fixed: the project name, undo and redo share the left tray |
| H8 | The count-in figure is 9 px in the toggle's corner and hard to read. | Open |

## Tokens and shared pieces

The fixes use existing tokens only (a tray is `controlLarge` plus `space.xs` all
round). Two shared pieces came out of them: `ui/Wells.h` (the strip's well, now
for any display) and `ui/components/AddSlot.h` (the dashed add slot).
